Planning Basis

Use net production hours, not paid shift length, if breaks/changeovers are intentionally excluded. Material storage positions are floor-planning estimates based on the selected coverage window—not warehouse inventory policy.

Rate & Daily Maximum Comparison

Storage & Handling Snapshot

Inbound line-side positions = ceil(skids/hour × inbound coverage hours), summed by material. Finished pallet buffer positions = ceil(pallets/hour × finished-goods coverage hours). A buffer position is one downstream floor location for a completed pallet waiting for removal, stretch-wrap/QA, or warehouse transfer. These are concurrent staging positions—not labor positions or daily moves.

Process Takt Chart

Each column is the effective process labor time per reference unit: selected core timing × process events/reference unit plus recurring auxiliary labor, divided by current positions. The horizontal takt line is 60 ÷ required reference units/min. A column above the line cannot sustain the required rate with the currently configured positions.
