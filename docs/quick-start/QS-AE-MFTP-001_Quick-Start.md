# Material Flow & Takt Planner — Quick Start

1. Export a compatible **Report JSON** from the Cycle Time & Labor Study.
2. Import the report into the planner.
3. Enter the required production rate and net production hours.
4. Select the material-flow rate basis.
5. Enter inbound and finished-goods coverage windows.
6. Enter cartons per finished pallet.
7. Review labor bottleneck, cartoner, belt/line, and constrained-rate signals.
8. Review the process takt chart.
9. Confirm material quantities and pieces per supply skid.
10. Review inbound skid positions and finished-pallet buffer positions.
11. Export the planning JSON, material CSV, or PDF-oriented report as required.
