# Material Flow & Takt Planner — Limitations

- Capacity depends on the quality of the imported timing study.
- Planning rate selection materially changes consumption and staging results.
- Coverage hours are floor-planning assumptions, not warehouse safety-stock policy.
- Mathematical position counts do not guarantee physical fit or material-handler access.
- Material master data such as pieces per supply skid must be verified.
- The planner does not replace layout review, ergonomic review, traffic analysis, safety analysis, or site-specific logistics standards.
